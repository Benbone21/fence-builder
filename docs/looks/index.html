Fence Builder: pick a look

Five looks for the app. Fence Builder now opens in the light Clean look, whatever the computer's own dark setting is. To change it: Settings → Look and text size. The look is kept on each computer, so the office computer and a laptop can each have their own. Click any picture to see it full size.

White and very light grey, dark grey words. Blue only on the main button of each page, the page you are on, and links.

The Builder's left bar

Less at once: the few things you need right then up top, and everything else in big labelled sections that start closed. A section you open stays open the next time. The line between the left bar and the map can now be dragged to make the bar wider (double-click it to put it back); that is remembered on the computer.

What changed in the layout

WhereBeforeAfter
ColoursFollowed the computer's dark setting, so it showed dark. Bright blue blocks on buttons, tabs, step numbers and the how-to cards.Opens light (Clean). Blue only on the main button of a page, the page you are on and links. Steps, tabs, switches and picked items use a light tint or an outline. Five looks to pick from in Settings.
Top barAll the page buttons looked the same, the page you were on was a solid blue block, and "Saved" sat between the two groups.Two clear groups: the job pages (Jobs, Builder, Final bill) with the job picker and "Saved" on the left, and the "Setup" pages (Fence types, Braces, Materials, Settings) on the right. The page you are on is white with a line under it. On a narrower window the setup group moves to a second row instead of squeezing; on a phone each group fills the width.
Map toolbar14 small buttons in two rows, with the search first.One row, in groups: Draw fence (big, blue), Add gate, Add brace | Undo, Redo | 3D view, Show (Rolls and Dimensions, with ticks) and More (Refresh, My location, Save area for offline, and Clear map in red at the bottom). Search is on the right. All buttons are at least 40 pixels tall.
Builder, setting upThe four brace switches open at the top, big totals, a long paragraph of drawing help, the lines list with "Clear map" in it.The job's two numbers and the Final bill button, then 1. Pick the kind of fence and 2. Draw it on the map. Drawing help is behind "How to draw". The lines list and "Braces added for you (4 of 4 on)" are sections that open and close. Clear map is in the map's More menu.
Builder, one lineJob totals and switches again, then everything about the line in one long column (rolls table, hills, ground, every soil, every point)."← All fence lines" and "Zoom to it", the line's name and fence type, then three key numbers (length, line posts, fenced in or steepest). Then sections that start closed: Posts and braces, Ground along this fence (its heading says the gist, like "Mostly easy digging."), Hills and height, Points on this line (opens by itself when you click a point on the map). The line's buttons are at the bottom.
Every pagePage titles, widths and card corners differed from page to page.The same page top everywhere: title and one line on the left, the main button on the right, a thin line under it. The same width, padding, card corners and spacing on every page.
Final billSix big buttons squeezed next to the title.Save estimate and Customer quote next to the title; Job details, Fence map, Copy list and Print my copy in a row just under it.
Jobs boardA solid blue "Open job" on every card."Open job" is outlined, so the board is calmer; the "+ New job" card is a light dashed box.
Job page"← All jobs" was a big button; the stage you were at was a solid blue block."← All jobs" is a plain link above the title; the stage you are at is tinted with a line under it.
How-to cardsBlue boxes.White cards with a thin coloured line down the side, smaller in the Builder's left bar. "Got it" still hides each one.

Nothing about the estimating, the prices or the saved jobs changed. The customer quote stays white in every look.
