Keyboard shortcuts

Map

FDraw a fence line
GAdd a gate, then click a line
BAdd a brace, then click a line or point
DeleteDelete the selected point
TChange the selected line's fence type
W A S D or arrowsMove the map (hold Shift to go faster)
MSwitch between the builder and the estimate
/Search the map

While drawing

ClickAdd a corner
Backspace or Ctrl+ZRemove the last point
Esc or Enter or FEnd the line here and keep it
Double-clickEnd the line here
GGate: your last point is one gate post, click where the other one goes
F after a gateBack to drawing fence

The Cancel button throws the line away.

Anywhere

Ctrl+ZUndo
Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+ZRedo
RAdd a typed-in run
1 to 7Switch pages
EscClose or deselect
